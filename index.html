THROUGH DEEP TIME

One Planet. Billions of Years. Countless Changes in Life.

Welcome to Through Deep Time, a journey across billions of years of Earth's history. From the formation of our planet to the rise and diversification of life, discover how changing environments, evolutionary developments, and mass extinctions shaped the biodiversity we see today.
